Gym Performance Manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026

What this app is

A private tool a personal trainer uses to assign exercises to their trainees and record the weight and repetitions each trainee should work at. It is not a public service and nothing is sold through it.

What is collected

  • Your name and email address, taken from your Google account when you sign in. Nothing else from Google is read, and no password is ever seen or stored.
  • Your training data — which exercises your trainer assigned you, the weight and repetitions they set, and the history of those numbers over time.

There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, and no cookies beyond the one that keeps you signed in.

Who can see it

  • You — your own exercises, numbers and history.
  • Your trainer — the same, for every trainee they coach, since setting those numbers is what the app is for.

Trainees cannot see each other's data. This is enforced by database access rules, not only by the app's screens.

Where it is stored

In a Supabase database, and served through Cloudflare. Sign-in is handled by Google. Technique videos may be stored alongside the app or embedded from YouTube, in which case YouTube's own privacy policy applies to playback. Your data is not sold, shared or sent anywhere else.

Deleting your data

Ask your trainer, or email ninjagur.dev@gmail.com. Removing your account deletes your profile, your assigned exercises and your entire history. This cannot be undone.

אין אנליטיקה, אין פרסום, אין מעקב, ואין עוגיות מלבד זו ששומרת אותך מחובר.

מתאמנים אינם יכולים לראות מידע של מתאמנים אחרים. הדבר נאכף בכללי גישה במסד הנתונים.
